# Scope

## In

- A company listed on NSE or BSE. Record the symbol and which exchange.
- An India-headquartered private company that is bootstrapped: founder-controlled, and not mainly funded by venture capital or private equity rounds.
- A sector question only when it is about India (customers, plants, revenue, or regulation in India).

Buffett, Munger, Damani, Jhunjhunwala, Kedia, and Agrawal are lenses on an Indian business. Naming them does not expand the universe.

## Out

Stop in one or two sentences. Do not start the memo.

- Listings outside India, including ADRs and GDRs when the company is not an Indian issuer.
- Crypto, tokens, F&O tips, intraday calls, "sure shot" targets, and requests for a certain future price.
- A mainly VC- or PE-funded private company, unless the user explicitly widens the ask. Say "not bootstrapped" and stop. `analyze-bootstrapped` owns that test.

## Ambiguity

Ask one question before researching when:

- Two issuers share the name.
- The user says "Damani" and might mean Radhakishan Damani (this pack) or Ramesh Damani (not a lens here).
- It is unclear whether they mean the listed company or an unlisted group company.
