# Andrade lens

Kenneth Andrade, Old Bridge. He buys capital efficiency when margins are low and treats a high return, while the leaders add capacity, as a cycle. He has said the style does not believe in quality at any price.

He has also said he cannot predict the market or the growth, that profit is the industry and capital employed is the management, and that dominance has to come with higher sales and lower capital employed. Use those sentences. Do not add a target return such as "double the risk-free rate" unless you cite the interview and still refuse to turn it into a target for this reader.

## Iron rules

1. India only. NSE, BSE, or an India bootstrapped company. Anything else: stop and say so.
2. Every figure has source, period, and consolidated or standalone. If it is not in a source, write "not found". Do not estimate silently.
3. INR, with crore or lakh stated. Indian fiscal year (April–March) unless the filing says otherwise.
4. Separate fact, inference, and opinion.
5. Promoter holding, pledge, related parties, and auditor notes are part of the analysis.
6. Close with this disclaimer. No buy, sell, or target presented as advice or as a certain outcome.

> This is an educational research memo, not investment advice, not a SEBI-registered recommendation, and not a solicitation to buy or sell any security. Figures are only as good as the filings cited. You decide what to do with them.

Listed facts come from `../analyze-financials/SKILL.md` if they are not already on the page. A leader comparison uses `../analyze-market-leader/SKILL.md` only when this test needs a sourced leader.

## Tests

1. Dominance and capital. A sourced share or a sourced lead, and capital employed from the same period. He wants higher sales and lower capital employed together. Share not found does not pass. A large revenue with no capital figure does not pass.
2. No forecast. Report ROCE from `statements.md` and say that definition already subtracts cash. Do not predict next year's profit. If the memo's reason is a growth forecast, fail this test.
3. Supply. Are the largest players adding capacity, and are they borrowing to do it? Only from filings you opened. A demand story or a TAM slide does not pass. Competitor capex not opened: not found.
4. Cycle, not quality at any price. Report the margin and ROE series you opened. His pattern is to buy low margins and low ROE and to treat high ROE as the other side of the cycle. "Exit" in that sentence is his description. It is not a sell instruction. A memo that accepts any multiple because the business is high quality, with no supply check, fails.
5. Price. Sourced close and multiple, or "not found".

## Kill

Fail when the case is quality at any price, or when the reason is a forecast of next year's growth.
