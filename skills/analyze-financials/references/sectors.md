# Sector lines

Read this after `statements.md`. It says which lines to pick up. It does not set a normal ratio, a healthy range, or a market size. If the filing does not print the line, write "not found".

## Jewellery

- Report gold-metal loans with borrowings. A working-capital sanction is not the loan balance.
- Customer advances and gold booked in a scheme are liabilities or stock, as the note describes them. They are not sales.
- Grams and rupee revenue stay in separate sentences. A gold-price move is not a moat.
- A company-printed P/E is not an exchange market cap. Use the price section in `statements.md`.

## Retail stores

- Lease liabilities follow `statements.md`. The format does not move them into net debt.
- New stores are not maintenance capex. Owner earnings stay not found until the note splits maintenance from growth.
- Do not infer the sign of working capital from the format.
- If two pages give two store counts, report both. Do not pick one.

## IT services

- Use the geography note for India revenue and for revenue outside India. If the note says "rest of the world" and does not split it, say that.
- Employee cost over revenue from operations, same statement, same year, only when both lines exist.
- Utilization, attrition, and offshoring mix only when the company prints them. Do not supply a normal rate.

## Pharmaceuticals

- Split formulations and API only when the segment note splits them.
- A plant observation, a warning letter, or an import alert is in the memo only when a filing or the regulator's page was opened. An unopened page is "not found", not a clean plant.

## Insurers

- Do not use inventory days or the non-financial ROCE in `statements.md`.
- Combined ratio, solvency ratio, embedded value, and the investment book only when the filing prints them.
- Float is unearned premium or a claims reserve on the balance sheet. Trade payables are not float. Do not invent a float earnings number.

## Real estate

- Use the company's own pre-sales or bookings figure only when its presentation or filing prints it, with area and value in separate sentences. Pre-sales are not revenue under Ind AS 115; revenue is recognised on completion or handover as the note says.
- Customer advances and inventory (land, work in progress) are balance-sheet lines. Read the inventory note for land at cost versus market.
- Joint-development and joint-venture projects: say whether the figure is the company's share or the project total.

## Public sector undertakings

- Government of India and state government holding come from the shareholding pattern, not the promoter line of a private company. Disinvestment news is not a filing.
- Dividend policy: the DIPAM guideline only when that page was opened; otherwise the company's own dividend distribution policy.
- Subsidies, administered prices, and receivables from government departments are separate lines when the note prints them. Do not net them.

## Capital goods and EPC

- Order book and order inflow only as the company prints them, with the date. An order book is not revenue.
- Contract assets (unbilled revenue) and retention money are in working capital. Report them; do not fold them into trade receivables unless the note does.
