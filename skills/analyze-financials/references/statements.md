## Which statements

From the annual report, for the same fiscal years:

- Consolidated and standalone, kept in separate rows. Never blend them.
- Statement of profit and loss, balance sheet, and cash flow.
- The auditor's report on those statements.
- The Ind AS 24 related-party note, contingent liabilities, and capital work-in-progress.

From the exchange, the latest shareholding pattern (promoter holding and pledge). It is not inside the P&L. If you did not open it, pledge is "not found", not zero.

Label audited annual versus unaudited or limited-review quarterly.

If the cash-flow statement's closing cash is described as net of overdrafts and it differs from the balance-sheet cash line, show the bridge or do not use the unreconciled line. If the net increase or decrease line does not bridge opening cash to closing cash, do not use that line. Use the operating, investing, and financing subtotals when those subtotals do bridge. A prior-year cell that is blank in what you read is "not found", not zero. A dash printed in the statement, where that statement uses a dash for no amount, is nil. A blank extraction is not a dash. If profit in the statement of changes in equity differs from profit in the P&L, show both. ROE uses profit attributable to owners.

## Ratios

Use these definitions. If a website uses another formula, record both and treat the filing-based one as the figure you discuss.

- ROE = PAT attributable to owners / average equity attributable to owners. Average means this year and last year. Same statement for both.
- ROCE = EBIT / average capital employed. Capital employed = equity + borrowings − cash and cash equivalents. Same statement for every term.
- Interest cover = EBIT / finance cost. If finance cost is zero or net interest is income, write "not meaningful".
- Net debt = current and non-current borrowings − cash and cash equivalents. State any other bank balance you also subtracted. Also report current and non-current lease liabilities when the balance sheet has them. Do not add leases into that net-debt line. A claim of little debt is unavailable until lease liabilities are reported or marked "not found".
- CFO / PAT = cash from operations / PAT. Same statement, same year.
- Other income share = other income / profit before tax.

EBIT is profit before exceptional items, tax, and finance cost. If you leave exceptionals in, say so. Do not annualize one quarter and call it ROCE.

## Working capital days

State the denominator.

- Receivable days = trade receivables / revenue from operations × 365.
- Inventory days = inventory / cost of goods sold × 365. If COGS is not disclosed, use cost of materials consumed plus purchases of stock-in-trade, and say that.
- Payable days = trade payables / the same cost base × 365.

Negative working capital (payables larger than inventory plus receivables) is a fact to report, not a compliment. Do not infer the sign from the industry. A grocery or retail format is not evidence that payables fund inventory.

## Notes that are not in the P&L

- Related parties: sales, purchases, loans, and guarantees with promoter entities. Give the amount and, when revenue is in the same statement, the share of revenue.
- Contingent liabilities: total and any single item that is large versus equity.
- Capital work-in-progress: balance and whether the note says projects are overdue.
- Auditor: firm name, a change in the last three years if the report or notice says so, qualifications, emphasis of matter, and key audit matters on revenue, inventory, or related parties.
- Shareholding pattern: promoter and promoter group percent. Pledge as a percent of promoter holding and, if the column exists, as a percent of total equity.

## Price

One close, one date. Prefer the NSE or BSE page. If you use the annual report's market-price table, say it is the company's reprint and cite the date or the high/low month it prints. A company-printed P/E is that printed multiple. It is not an exchange market cap. Do not multiply a P/E by earnings to invent one.

When the source gives them, report the close and date, the market cap, and the multiple that same source prints. If you compute price / EPS, show both numbers and the EPS period. Do not put a quarterly EPS under an annual multiple.

No target. Whether the multiple is attractive is a lens opinion, not a figure in this section. If neither the exchange page nor the filing has a price, write "not found". A split, bonus, or other action between two closes is `../../analyze-actions/SKILL.md`. Do not compare those closes as if the share were unchanged.

## Governance series

When you opened three shareholding patterns or three annual reports, report promoter and promoter group percent, pledge, the auditor's name, and related-party sales or loans for each year. One year is allowed if you say the earlier years were not opened. The series is then "not found". Do not describe a single year as flat.

Pledge comes from the exchange Regulation 31 file. An annual-report cell that prints a dash or Nil is what that table prints. It is not the Regulation 31 figure. If Regulation 31 was not opened, pledge is "not found", not zero. A bulk deal, a block deal, or a takeover disclosure is not this pledge figure. That method is `../../analyze-deals/SKILL.md`.

## Sectors

Jewellery, retail stores, IT services, pharmaceuticals, and insurers: read `sectors.md` for which lines to lift. That note sets no normal ratio.

## Flags

Raise a flag, as a fact about the statements, when:

- CFO / PAT is below 1 in more than one of the years you opened, while PAT rose.
- Other income is a large share of profit before tax. State the share. Do not use a cutoff you invented as if it were a standard.
- Pledge is disclosed and above zero. State the percent.
- The auditor qualified the report, or a key audit matter matches a related-party or revenue concern.
- Net debt and interest cover show the finance cost consumes a large share of EBIT. State the cover. Do not call it "high debt" without the figures.

A flag is not a buy or sell opinion.
