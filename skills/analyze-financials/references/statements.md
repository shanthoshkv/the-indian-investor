## Which statements

From the annual report, for the same fiscal years:

- Consolidated and standalone, kept in separate rows. Never blend them.
- Statement of profit and loss, balance sheet, and cash flow.
- The auditor's report on those statements.
- The Ind AS 24 related-party note, contingent liabilities, and capital work-in-progress.

From the exchange, the latest shareholding pattern (promoter holding and pledge). It is not inside the P&L. If you did not open it, pledge is "not found", not zero.

Label audited annual versus unaudited or limited-review quarterly.

## Ratios

Use these definitions. If a website uses another formula, record both and treat the filing-based one as the figure you discuss.

- ROE = PAT attributable to owners / average equity attributable to owners. Average means this year and last year. Same statement for both.
- ROCE = EBIT / average capital employed. Capital employed = equity + borrowings − cash and cash equivalents. Same statement for every term.
- Interest cover = EBIT / finance cost. If finance cost is zero or net interest is income, write "not meaningful".
- Net debt = current and non-current borrowings − cash and cash equivalents. State any other bank balance you also subtracted.
- CFO / PAT = cash from operations / PAT. Same statement, same year.
- Other income share = other income / profit before tax.

EBIT is profit before exceptional items, tax, and finance cost. If you leave exceptionals in, say so. Do not annualize one quarter and call it ROCE.

## Working capital days

State the denominator.

- Receivable days = trade receivables / revenue from operations × 365.
- Inventory days = inventory / cost of goods sold × 365. If COGS is not disclosed, use cost of materials consumed plus purchases of stock-in-trade, and say that.
- Payable days = trade payables / the same cost base × 365.

Negative working capital (payables larger than inventory plus receivables) is a fact to report, not a compliment.

## Notes that are not in the P&L

- Related parties: sales, purchases, loans, and guarantees with promoter entities. Give the amount and, when revenue is in the same statement, the share of revenue.
- Contingent liabilities: total and any single item that is large versus equity.
- Capital work-in-progress: balance and whether the note says projects are overdue.
- Auditor: firm name, a change in the last three years if the report or notice says so, qualifications, emphasis of matter, and key audit matters on revenue, inventory, or related parties.
- Shareholding pattern: promoter and promoter group percent. Pledge as a percent of promoter holding and, if the column exists, as a percent of total equity.

## Flags

Raise a flag, as a fact about the statements, when:

- CFO / PAT is below 1 in more than one of the years you opened, while PAT rose.
- Other income is a large share of profit before tax. State the share. Do not use a cutoff you invented as if it were a standard.
- Pledge is disclosed and above zero. State the percent.
- The auditor qualified the report, or a key audit matter matches a related-party or revenue concern.
- Net debt and interest cover show the finance cost consumes a large share of EBIT. State the cover. Do not call it "high debt" without the figures.

A flag is not a buy or sell opinion.
