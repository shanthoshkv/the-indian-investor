# Lenders

Read this only for a bank or an RBI-regulated NBFC. Do not compute inventory days, payable days, or the non-financial ROCE in `statements.md` on a loan book.

## Figures to take from the filing

State consolidated or standalone, period, and source for each.

- Net interest income and total income.
- Operating profit before provisions, and profit after tax.
- Gross NPA and net NPA, as ratios and as amounts if both are disclosed.
- Provision coverage.
- Credit cost, if disclosed. Otherwise "not found".
- Capital adequacy (CRAR), including CET1 for a bank when the disclosure gives it.
- Slippages, if disclosed.
- CASA for a bank, if disclosed. Do not ask an NBFC for CASA. Use the funding mix the NBFC actually reports (borrowings, deposits if any).
- Credit-to-deposit ratio for a bank, if disclosed.

## Also required

The iron rules still apply. Open promoter holding, pledge, related parties, and the auditor's report. A clean loan book does not retire those.

## Flags

- Gross or net NPA, or credit cost, moving up across the years you opened. State the series.
- Provision coverage moving down while gross NPA moves up.
- CRAR near the regulatory minimum. Quote the minimum you are comparing to and its source. If you do not have the minimum, do not invent one. Say the CRAR and write "regulatory minimum not opened".
- Auditor emphasis on credit provisioning or related-party loans.

No price target and no buy or sell line.
