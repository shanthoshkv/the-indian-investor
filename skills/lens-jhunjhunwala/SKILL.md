# Jhunjhunwala lens

A call on long-term earnings power in a large Indian opportunity. This lens is more willing to pay for growth than the Damani lens. When both ran, that disagreement stays in the memo. Do not average it.

## Iron rules

1. India only. NSE, BSE, or an India bootstrapped company. Anything else: stop and say so.
2. Every figure has source, period, and consolidated or standalone. If it is not in a source, write "not found". Do not estimate silently.
3. INR, with crore or lakh stated. Indian fiscal year (April–March) unless the filing says otherwise.
4. Separate fact, inference, and opinion.
5. Promoter holding, pledge, related parties, and auditor notes are part of the analysis.
6. Close with this disclaimer. No buy, sell, or target presented as advice or as a certain outcome.

> This is an educational research memo, not investment advice, not a SEBI-registered recommendation, and not a solicitation to buy or sell any security. Figures are only as good as the filings cited. You decide what to do with them.

Listed facts come from `../analyze-financials/SKILL.md` if they are not already on the page. Private facts come from `../analyze-bootstrapped/SKILL.md`.

## Tests

1. India getting richer, or a company-specific edge. Say which, and the fact that supports it (volumes, a distribution reach, a cost gap). "India story" with no unit economics is a fail.
2. Unit economics exist. A margin, a ROCE, or a per-store or per-unit figure from a filing or the company's own published number. Not found is not a pass. Missing unit economics fails test 1.
3. What proves the thesis wrong. One observable fact (a margin, a share, a regulation, a capex payback), not a mood.
4. Price for growth. State the sourced multiple and the sourced multi-year growth. This lens can accept a higher multiple than Damani when test 1 and test 2 passed. That sentence is an opinion. It is not a target and not permission to skip the multiple.
5. Conviction shape. This lens prefers a few names the tests support, not a long list of India stories. Say whether this business is that kind of name. Do not tell the reader how to size a portfolio.

## Kill

Fail when the write-up is an India story and test 2 is not found or failed.
