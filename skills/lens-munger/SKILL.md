# Munger lens

Inversion and misjudgment. Do not repeat the Buffett tests under another name.

## Iron rules

1. India only. NSE, BSE, or an India bootstrapped company. Anything else: stop and say so.
2. Every figure has source, period, and consolidated or standalone. If it is not in a source, write "not found". Do not estimate silently.
3. INR, with crore or lakh stated. Indian fiscal year (April–March) unless the filing says otherwise.
4. Separate fact, inference, and opinion.
5. Promoter holding, pledge, related parties, and auditor notes are part of the analysis.
6. Close with this disclaimer. No buy, sell, or target presented as advice or as a certain outcome.

> This is an educational research memo, not investment advice, not a SEBI-registered recommendation, and not a solicitation to buy or sell any security. Figures are only as good as the filings cited. You decide what to do with them.

Listed facts come from `../analyze-financials/SKILL.md` if they are not already on the page. Private facts come from `../analyze-bootstrapped/SKILL.md`.

## Tests

1. How this dies in India over ten years. Three paths, each tagged inference, each tied to a fact (a customer, a regulation, a plant, a promoter, a commodity). "Competition increases" with no named rival is not a path.
2. Bias in the bull case. Name one: social proof (a popular NSE name), incentive-caused bias (the promoter's narrative), deprival (fear of missing a move that already happened), or authority (a famous shareholder). Quote the claim that carries it. If the memo has no bull case yet, say so and skip. Do not invent a straw man.
3. Lollapalooza or one ratio. Several independent facts pointing the same way, or the whole memo resting on one multiple. Say which.
4. Quality at a fair price. A low multiple on a weak business is a fail on this lens. Weak means the moat test or the cash-versus-profit flag already failed. A high multiple on a strong business is not an automatic fail. It goes to test 3.

## Kill

Fail the lens when the case needs a forecast of next year's multiple and nothing else.
