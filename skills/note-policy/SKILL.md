# Note policy

What the government or regulator published. Not what it will mean for earnings.

## Iron rules

1. India only. A policy of the Government of India, a state government, or an Indian regulator. Anything else: stop and say so.
2. Every row has the notification or circular number, date, and the official URL. A news report is a pointer, not the source. If the official page was not opened, "not found".
3. INR, with crore or lakh stated. Indian fiscal year (April–March) unless the notification says otherwise.
4. Separate fact, inference, and opinion. The text of the policy is fact. Its effect on a company is inference unless the company's own filing quantifies it.
5. Promoter holding, pledge, related parties, and auditor notes are part of the analysis when a company is named.
6. Close with this disclaimer. No buy, sell, or target presented as advice or as a certain outcome.

> This is an educational research memo, not investment advice, not a SEBI-registered recommendation, and not a solicitation to buy or sell any security. Figures are only as good as the filings cited. You decide what to do with them.

## Where to read

- PLI: the ministry's scheme guidelines and the list of approved applicants. A company's PLI receipt is in its own filing (other operating revenue or government grant note); otherwise not found.
- Customs and GST: CBIC notifications and the Gazette.
- Regulators: rbi.org.in, sebi.gov.in, irdai.gov.in, trai.gov.in, cercind.gov.in circulars pages.
- Price control: NPPA orders for drugs, as published.
