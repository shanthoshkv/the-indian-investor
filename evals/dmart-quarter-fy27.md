The limited-review label is that letter. The XBRL label is Unaudited. They are stated separately. This memo does not upgrade Unaudited into audited.

Filing dates on the extract: board meeting 10-10-2026, prior intimation 01-10-2026, financial year 01-04-2026 to 31-03-2027, scrip 540376, NSE symbol DMART.
