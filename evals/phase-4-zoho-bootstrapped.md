# Zoho Corporation — bootstrapped memo

Phase 4 dry run. One private company. The funding gate is applied first. The run stops on headquarters.

## Scope

Private. India headquarters was not established from the pages opened, so the memo stops before unit economics. The company is not given a revenue, a margin, or a valuation.

Pages opened:

- https://www.zoho.com/ourstory.html
- https://www.zoho.com/news/zoho-celebrates-100-milliion-users.html (company press release, Austin, 5 September 2023)

MCA was not opened. Charges are "MCA not opened". That is not "no charges".

Funding, from those pages: the company says it does not take venture capital. Our Story says "Privately held, profitable, and proud to be" and "Our decision not to take venture capital, not to go for an IPO, and not to be acquired". The 5 September 2023 press release says Zoho is "the first bootstrapped SaaS company" to reach the user milestone it announces, and that it has grown "without ever taking external funding". That is the company's statement. It is not an MCA search, and it is not a cap table.

Headquarters, from those pages: Our Story says the idea was born in a small apartment in the suburbs of Chennai, and the page carries the line "Made in India. Made for the World." The 5 September 2023 press release says "Headquartered in Austin, Texas, with international headquarters in Chennai, India". Both sentences are the company's. They do not show a single India headquarters, and they do not show that most of the operations are in India. The skill stops when that is not shown. This memo stops here.

A venture or private-equity round was not the funding story on the pages opened. The stop is the headquarters test, not a finding that the company is venture-funded.

It is not an INR figure and it is not a filing. It is not the revenue.

## Unknowns

This list is the point of the stop. It is longer than the evidence table.

- India registered office, CIN, and date of incorporation. MCA was not opened.
- Whether most operations, staff, or revenue are in India. Not found.
- Founder shareholding, and whether founder control is published. Not found.
- Charges on assets. MCA was not opened. That is not a finding of no charges.
- Disputes, defaults, or a public record that would answer a lend-to-founder question. Not found. Silence is not a pass.
- Revenue in INR, for any year, from the company or from a filing. Not found. The 2023 US-dollar sentence is not the figure.
- Profit, margin, retention, and customer concentration. Not found. "Profitable" on Our Story is not a profit figure.
- India revenue as a share of the total. Not found. A global revenue line would not pass an India-only test even if it were used.
- Auditor, related parties, and pledge. Not found. Pledge is the wrong instrument for this private company until a charge or a filing is opened.
- Current employees, users, and countries. The page counters were zero when opened. The 2023 press-release counts are not used as the current figure.
- Any listed company's multiple as a value for this company. Not used. An analogy was not built.

No unit economics follow the headquarters stop. No valuation is stated.
