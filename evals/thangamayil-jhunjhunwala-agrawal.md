## Lens: Jhunjhunwala

- India or company edge: Not found as a company edge. Rupee revenue rose 73.1% and grams rose 12.3%. The directors, in the Phase 4 memo, tie the rupee increase to gold and silver prices. That is a price fact, not an India-getting-richer fact and not a distribution moat.
- Unit economics: Found for FY26. ROCE 27.77% on the skill definition in the Phase 4 memo. One year. The test does not fail for a missing margin.
- What proves it wrong: Grams stop rising while rupee sales keep rising only with the gold price, or the FY27 outlet plan in the Phase 4 memo is missed. Inference. No forecast of next year's profit is made here.
- Multiple versus growth: The printed multiple is 30.28 on the company's 31 March quote. The exchange close is not found. The growth that is sourced is one step, and most of the rupee step is not the gram step. A higher multiple than Damani is not asserted. Damani did not run.
- Conviction shape: Not stated. This lens refuses a portfolio weight.
- Refuses to opine on: a target, a buy, and whether 30.28 is cheap.

## Lens: Agrawal (QGLP)

- Quality: Not passed. FY26 ROE 27.93% and ROCE 27.77% are from the filings opened in Phase 4. FY25 ROE on the skill definition is 14.88%. FY25 ROCE was not found. A third year of primary statements was not opened. No sourced share of Indian jewellery demand was opened. Three years and one competitive fact are the minimum when the filings exist. The older highlight years were not those statements.
- Growth: The window is two company-statement years, not three. Revenue 73.1% and PAT 196.2% are that one step. The test is not passed on a longer window.
- Longevity: Not passed. An Indian jewellery size in INR was not found in Phase 4. The gram gap against the rupee gap is a reason not to treat 73.1% as a volume runway. Inference.
- Price versus growth: Not found. The exchange close was not opened. The printed 30.28 is not that close, and the growth window is one step.
- Refuses to opine on: a target, and any Coffee Can pass. Ten years were not opened.

## Where the lenses disagree

Jhunjhunwala has a FY26 ROCE, so the unit-economics test does not fail. Agrawal does not pass quality or growth on a two-year window. Neither lens has an exchange close. They are not averaged.

## Critic

- Breaks the thesis: Using ₹4,148.20 from a broker page as the 31 March quote, or calling 30.28 an exchange market cap, would break. The memo does not do either.
- Weakens it: Regulation 31 is still unopened. The growth window is two years. FY25 ROCE is not found. Titan was the size comparison in Phase 4 and is not restated as a share.
- Nit: none.

## Revised conclusion

Do not pass QGLP, and do not treat the printed P/E as a price for growth. FY26 company ROCE is 27.77%. Revenue growth of 73.1% sits next to gram growth of 12.3%. The exchange close on a named date was not opened.
