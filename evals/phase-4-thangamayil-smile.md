## Business

The company makes and sells gold, silver, diamond, and platinum jewellery. Note 23 is sale of those products. Export sales in the directors' report are a dash for both years. The directors' report says the company opened 10 outlets in FY 2025-26, that Chennai and its surroundings contributed nearly 20% of revenue on an annualised basis, and that a second expansion phase in the Chennai metro is the plan for FY 2026-27.

The highlights page prints 58 branches in leased property and 8 in owned property at the end of FY 2025-26, against 52 and 8 a year earlier. That is a net addition of 6 leased branches. The directors say 10 outlets were opened. The two counts were not reconciled in the pages read. Both are reported. Neither is adjusted.

The directors say the revenue increase was attributable to gold and silver price escalation, and that Chennai also contributed. Note 42 quantifies grams. Those two facts are kept apart below. A higher gold price is not treated as a moat.

## Lens: Kedia

- Fit (smaller listed company): The company is listed. It is not Titan. Titan's audited consolidated column for the year ended 31 March 2026 shows sale of products and services ₹76,797 crore and other operating revenue ₹10,787 crore. Those two lines add to ₹87,584 crore. Total income in that column is ₹88,136 crore, which also includes other income of ₹552 crore. Titan standalone sale of products and services ₹67,384 crore plus other operating revenue ₹10,170 crore adds to ₹77,554 crore. Thangamayil's company revenue from operations is ₹8,499.33 crore. The statement types differ, so those rupees are not divided into a share. On either Titan column, Thangamayil's revenue is the smaller one. S does not fail for being the sector leader. The screen is not stopped for that reason.
- Small (S): not found. A share of the Indian jewellery market was not found, because the INR market size was not found. An NSE or BSE market cap on a named date was not opened. The company's printed P/E of 30.28 was not turned into one. Revenue next to Titan's revenue shows which listing is larger. It is not the sourced share, and it is not a sourced market cap.
- Medium experience (MI): Tenure is found. Balarama Govinda Das has been Managing Director since 3 May 2001, and the two joint managing directors have been on the board since 2001. That is longer than a first-time promoter. The five profit-before-tax numbers on the highlights page are all profits, so a loss year was not found there. FY26 is described by the directors as a year of sharp gold and silver price moves and a marginal drop in volume at same-store outlets, while profit rose. That year is not treated as a cutoff. The clean-record half of this illustration is not found. SEBI orders were not opened. The exchange pledge file was not opened. MI is not a pass.
- Large aspiration (L): Evidenced, with two counts that the report itself does not reconcile. The directors' report says the company plans to open at least nine outlets in FY 2026-27, that phase II is to be completed by 31 December 2026, and that working-capital sanctions of ₹1,412 crore are already in place. The same annual report's expansion table prints an FY27 outlook of 8 new stores. A sentence that puts the FY26 outlay at "Rs.78,298 lakhs Crores" mixes units and is not used. The plan that is used is the outlet count and the sanctioned working-capital line.
- Extra-large Indian market (E): does not pass. The customer is in India, and exports are nil. An INR size of the Indian jewellery market, with a named publisher, was not found. The USD 49 billion sentence in the management discussion does not pass this test. Size not found does not pass.
- Sit tight (only if all four evidenced): not added. S is not found, MI is not a pass, and E does not pass.
- Refuses to opine on: a price, a multiple, and whether the reader should buy or sell. The exchange close was not opened.

## Critic

- Breaks the thesis: A "sit tight" line would break. S and E are not evidenced, and MI is not a pass. The memo does not contain that line. Dividing Thangamayil's company revenue by Titan's consolidated revenue and calling the result a market share would also break. That division is not in the memo.
- Weakens it: The appointment dates sit in a non-disqualification certificate, not in a biography of a downcycle. SEBI orders were not searched. Regulation 31 was not opened. Note 30's interest split does not add to the finance-cost total, so the cover uses the total only. The 10 outlets in the directors' report and the net 6 leased branches on the highlights page are both printed and not reconciled. Titan is a later results filing's audited column, not Titan's annual report.
- Nit: Note 17's component lines add to ₹51.40 crore and the printed non-current borrowings total is ₹51.41 crore. Note 43's inventory heads add to ₹2,953.62 crore against a balance-sheet inventory of ₹2,953.63 crore.

Checked: company figures are not mixed into Titan's consolidated total. The quarterly Titan columns are not in any ratio. Other income and cash from operations versus PAT are stated. The pledge dash is not written as zero. No sentence tells the reader to buy, sell, or expect a price.

## Revised conclusion

Do not add "sit tight". The company is a listed Tamil Nadu jeweller, the managing director has been on the board since 3 May 2001, and the annual report states an outlet plan for FY 2026-27. Revenue of ₹8,499.33 crore is smaller than Titan's audited revenue lines for the same year. That does not establish a share of the Indian market, and no exchange market cap was opened. Cash from operations is below profit, inventory days are 144 on the cost base above, and customer advances do not cover inventory.
