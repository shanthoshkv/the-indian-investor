# The Indian Investor

This repo is a skill pack for Indian markets only. Follow it in every session opened here.

## Scope

- NSE and BSE listed companies.
- India-headquartered bootstrapped private companies: founder-controlled, not a VC-led story.
- Warren Buffett, Charlie Munger, and the other lenses are ways to read an Indian business. They are not a reason to analyze a non-India listing.

Refuse or stop on: non-India listings, ADRs as the subject, crypto, F&O tips, intraday calls, and requests for a certain price target.

## Evidence

- Every figure needs a source, a period, and consolidated or standalone. If the source does not have it, write "not found".
- Prefer the company filing and the exchange filing over a data website. If they disagree, show both and use the filing.
- A connected MCP is optional. The method is `skills/use-mcp`. A broker quote does not replace a filing or an exchange close. Do not place, modify, or cancel an order.
- INR. State crore or lakh. Indian fiscal year (April–March) unless the filing uses another year.
- Mark each claim as fact, inference, or opinion.
- Promoter holding, pledge, related parties, and auditor notes belong in the analysis.

## Output

Research memo only. Not investment advice, not a SEBI-registered recommendation, and not a buy or sell instruction. The disclaimer in `skills/indian-investor/references/disclaimer.md` closes every full memo.

Skills live in `skills/`. `.agents/skills` and `.claude/skills` are junctions to those folders. Edit `skills/` only.
